Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 27, 2026 | Last Updated: August 27, 2026

Schedretary ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how your data is handled.

This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how we use it, and the measures we take to safeguard it when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Calendar & Schedule Details: Appointment times, daily time slots, weekly/monthly calendar organization metrics, and overall operational schedule records.
  • Client & Check-In Tracking: Names, appointment booking details, checking timestamps, and client active attendance logs.
  • Attendance History: Operational records of completed sessions, check-in data, and schedule completion logs.
  • Device & Usage Data (non-personal): App version, device type, crash logs, and anonymized usage metrics collected via third-party tools for performance optimization.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Manage master calendar booking pipelines, scheduling rules, and appointment availability blocks.
  • Process real-time schedule adjustments and deliver matching layout synchronization across paired client app variants.
  • Keep flawless operational records through custom completion histories and manual check-in tools.
  • Improve app stability, patch bugs, and optimize background features for performance enhancements.

3. Data Security & Encryption

We implement strict administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. All data transmitted between the app, connected client variants, and our backend systems is fully encrypted in transit using industry-standard secure protocols (such as HTTPS and TLS). Personal configurations and operational logs are stored securely within the app’s environment or on your local device. Sensitive actions are fully protected by strict access control mechanisms to avoid unauthorized disclosure.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Your information remains within the app environment to support core feature interactions. Third-party tools (e.g., analytics or crash reporting) may collect non-personal data to help us improve performance. These tools operate under strict privacy agreements and do not access identifiable user information.

5. User Control & Data Rights

  • Delete or Reset Profile: You may delete your data or reset your system configurations at any time via app settings.
  • Data Requests: You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data by reaching out to us.
  • Consent Management: You may opt out of non-essential data collection (e.g., analytics tools) via your app settings panel.

6. Third-Party Services

Schedretary may integrate third-party services for analytics and crash reporting. These services collect non-personal data only and are governed strictly by their own specific privacy terms.

7. Children’s Privacy

Schedretary is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with a revised “Effective Date” and communicated via in-app notifications or update logs.
