Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 23, 2026

Badminton Buddy (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how your information is handled. This policy explains what information is collected and how it is used when you use the Badminton Buddy mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect information you provide, information created while using the app, and limited device or service information needed to operate and secure the app.

  • Account and support information: Firebase account identifier, email address and verification status, support category and message, and a device or session identifier used to enforce single-device sign-in.
  • Queue Master information: Player names and aliases, optional contact details, profile preferences, avatar selections, player rank, queue and court details, match records, participation, timestamps, game durations, and win/loss statistics.
  • Tournament information: Tournament name, venue, date and description; event formats and categories; registered player names, ages, genders and ranks; court and umpire assignments; match schedules, scores, outcomes, standings, and result-review records.
  • Purchase and subscription information: Product and plan identifiers, purchase verification data, access entitlements, subscription state, renewal status, and start or expiration dates. Purchase tokens are sent to our server for verification. Google Play processes payments; we do not receive or store your payment-card or bank-account details.
  • Device and advertising information: App version, device type, diagnostic information, and advertising identifiers or ad interaction information processed by Google AdMob and related platform services.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide Queue Master and Tournament Master features, including queues, player records, events, match schedules, scoring, standings, umpire review, and public tournament viewing when enabled by the tournament owner.
  • Display statistics, performance summaries, game history, and participation-based court or shuttle cost calculations.
  • Authenticate accounts, maintain the single-device session, synchronize eligible records, verify purchases, provide support, and protect the service from misuse.
  • Serve ads and administer optional rewarded-ad features for eligible accounts.

3. Data Storage and Security

Guest records are stored only on the device, are not synchronized to our server, and are cleared when guest access is exited or at the next 4 a.m. cleanup. For signed-in accounts, records are saved locally first and eligible changes are synchronized with Firebase Realtime Database.

Free accounts can synchronize Queue Master operational records. Queue Master game history, player history, and temporary or ad-capped reward data may remain local. Tournament records may synchronize for signed-in free and paid accounts. Guest tournament data remains local. Available features and limits depend on the account plan.

When you sign out, the app tries to synchronize records eligible for your account’s current access plan before clearing local account records. If an eligible synchronization fails, sign-out is stopped and local records are retained. Records excluded from synchronization, such as free Queue Master game and player history, may not be on the server and are removed from the device when local account data is cleared. Signing out does not delete records stored in Firebase.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including secure network connections and access controls, to protect information. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share data with service providers as needed to operate the app: Google Firebase provides authentication, database synchronization, and server functions; Google Play processes purchases and subscriptions; and Google AdMob serves ads to eligible users. These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

If a tournament owner publishes a tournament, people with its active viewing code can view the information included in the public view. This may include the tournament name, venue, date and description; event categories; player or team names; match schedules, scores, outcomes and winners; court names; and standings. The owner can turn off sharing. Information viewers copied or saved while the tournament was published may remain outside our control.

A tournament owner may assign an umpire to a match. The umpire receives the match information needed to score it and submit a result for review. Tournament owners are responsible for having an appropriate basis and permission to enter and share information about other players and umpires.

5. Your Choices and Data Requests

  • You may request access to or correction of your personal information, or request its deletion or blocking, by choosing “Delete my account and all my data” in Contact Us or by emailing ali.asghar.akmad@gmail.com. We may ask you to verify your identity before acting on a request.
  • We retain signed-in account records while needed to provide the service and until a verified deletion request is completed, except for limited records we must retain for legal, security, accounting, or dispute-resolution purposes. Support requests are retained while needed to respond and maintain a record of the request. Guest data is cleared on exit or at the next 4 a.m. cleanup.
  • Where applicable, you may object to processing or withdraw consent, and you may lodge a complaint with the relevant privacy regulator. Some records may remain necessary to provide a feature you use or to meet legal obligations.
  • You can manage advertising preferences through the settings available on your device and Google account.

6. Third-Party Services

The app uses Firebase services, Google Play Billing and related purchase-verification services, and Google AdMob for app operations, purchase processing, and advertising. Each service is governed by its own privacy policy and terms.

7. Children’s Privacy

Badminton Buddy accounts are not intended for children under 13. Tournament organizers may enter information about youth participants, including age, as needed to administer an event. Organizers are responsible for providing any required notice and obtaining any required permission from a parent or guardian. Contact us if you believe information has been submitted inappropriately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with a revised effective date and communicated through in-app notices or update logs where appropriate.
